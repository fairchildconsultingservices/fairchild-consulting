Welcome to Dingus Eggs! 🥚

The basics:

  1. Add goals — one-time projects or daily habits.
  2. Complete them to earn Dingus Eggs (small, big, huge).
  3. Hatch eggs to win pets, weapons, vehicles, accessories. Bigger eggs = better odds at rare items.
  4. Equip items to make them appear in The Hatchery — your living warehouse where your avatar walks around with their loot.
  5. Visit the Egg Marketplace in The Hatchery to trade up small eggs for bigger ones with better drop odds.
  6. Customize your chibi avatar — face, hair, outfit, all of it.
  7. Build streaks (daily habits 7 days running = bonus big egg).
  8. Level up by earning XP. Unlock achievements along the way.

All data saves to your browser — refresh anytime, your progress sticks. Export a backup from Settings if you want.
