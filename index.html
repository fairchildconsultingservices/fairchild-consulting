Common questions

What people usually ask first

The three questions I get on almost every discovery call — answered up front so you can decide if a call is worth your time.

What if I don't know exactly what I want?

Most clients don't on day one — that's normal. The discovery call's job is to figure out the actual problem behind the problem and decide whether a tool, an automation, or just a better page is the right answer. No obligation either way.

Will I be able to maintain this without you?

Yes. You own the code, the keys, the docs, and a walkthrough recording so future-you (or your collaborator) can pick it back up. No hostage software. No subscription traps.

What if it doesn't work?

Fixed-price projects include 30 days of bug fixes after delivery at no extra cost. If we can't agree on what "done" looks like during the scoping call, we don't start.
